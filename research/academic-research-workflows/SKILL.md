# Academic Research Workflows

Use this skill for systematic academic research, literature reviews, proposal writing, or paper editing.

## Core Workflows
1. **Socratic Literature Survey:** Formulate clear research questions, map concepts, and trace citations back to primary sources.
2. **Methodology Auditing:** Evaluate the robustness of datasets, mathematical proofs, and statistical significance in papers.
3. **PRISMA Systematic Review:** Follow the PRISMA guidelines for screening and selecting eligible academic studies.
4. **Drafting Proposals:** Frame research questions, design methodologies, and highlight contributions clearly.
