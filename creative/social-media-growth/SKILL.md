## When to Use
Grow audience and engagement on social platforms. Use when building a personal brand, launching a company presence, scaling organic reach, or planning a social-first content strategy across Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube.

## Core Concepts
- **Platform-native content**: What works on LinkedIn fails on TikTok. Adapt format, tone, and length per platform.
- **Engagement loops**: Create content that invites replies, shares, and saves. Comments signal the algorithm.
- **Content ratios**: 80/20 rule — 80% value content, 20% promotional. Some use 4-1-1 (4 value, 1 soft sell, 1 hard sell).
- **Hook → Retain → Reward**: Every post has 3 seconds to hook, 10 seconds to retain, and delivers value at the end.
- **Thread/carousel mechanics**: Multi-part content keeps users on your post longer, boosting algorithmic ranking.
- **Cross-platform repurposing**: One core idea → 5+ platform-native formats. Don't copy-paste.

## Workflow
1. **Audit current state**: Follower count, engagement rate, top-performing posts, posting frequency, audience demographics.
2. **Define 3 content pillars**: Educational, entertaining, and personal/opinion. Map topics to each.
3. **Set cadence**: Twitter/X: 1-3x/day. LinkedIn: 3-5x/week. Instagram: 3-5x/week. TikTok: 1-2x/day. YouTube: 1-2x/week.
4. **Batch content creation**: Dedicate 2 hours weekly to write/schedule. Use templates for repeatable formats.
5. **Engage 30 min/day**: Comment on 10+ posts in your niche. Reply to every comment on your posts.
6. **Analyze weekly**: Track reach, engagement rate, saves/shares (not just likes). Identify top formats.
7. **Iterate monthly**: Double down on what works. Drop what doesn't. Test one new format per month.

## Key Patterns
```markdown
# High-Performing Post Formats by Platform

## Twitter/X Thread Template
Hook (must stop the scroll):
"Most [industry] people get [topic] wrong. Here's what actually works:"

Body (10-15 tweets):
- Each tweet = one idea
- Use numbers and specifics
- Add screenshots/data where possible
- End each tweet with a micro-hook to the next

Closer:
"If this was helpful, retweet tweet 1 and follow me for more [topic]."

## LinkedIn Post Template (Carousel or Text)
Hook line (one sentence, bold, polarizing if possible):
"I spent $10K on [X]. Here are 5 lessons:"

Body:
1. Lesson with specific example
2. Lesson with data point
3. Lesson with personal story
4. Lesson with contrarian take
5. Lesson with actionable next step

CTA: "Which lesson resonated most? Comment below."
```

## Key Metrics
| Platform | Good Engagement Rate | Viral Threshold | Optimal Post Length |
|----------|---------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Twitter/X | 1-3% | 5%+ | 100-280 chars |
| LinkedIn | 2-4% | 6%+ | 150-300 words |
| Instagram | 3-6% | 8%+ | 150-200 word caption |
| TikTok | 5-10% | 15%+ | 15-60 seconds |
| YouTube | 5-10% CTR | 12%+ CTR | 8-15 min videos |

## Pitfalls
- **Posting and ghosting**: Create content then never engage with comments. The algorithm punishes this.
- **Chasing trends over strategy**: Trending audio/topics bring views but not followers. Balance both.
- **Ignoring analytics**: What you feel works ≠ what data shows works. Review weekly.
- **Perfection paralysis**: A good post published beats a perfect post drafted. Ship > polish.
- **Buying followers**: Kills engagement rate, destroys algorithmic trust, attracts bots not buyers.

## Verification
- Engagement rate above platform average for your follower count
- Follower growth trajectory: consistent upward trend, not plateau
- Saves and shares (high-intent signals) trending up month-over-month
- Content is driving traffic to your website/products (UTM tracking)
- Comments are substantive (not just emojis) — signals genuine audience connection
