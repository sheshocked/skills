# Anti Slop Prose Writing

## When to Use
Use when editing AI generated text, emails, readmes, and marketing documents to make them sound human, deliberate, and clean.

## Prerequisites
- Standard text editor.

## Workflow
1. Prune structural AI padding (e.g. "Sure, here is X", "In conclusion").
2. Strip generic buzzwords: `delve`, `tapestry`, `testament`, `revolutionize`, `fostering`.
3. Simplify complex sentence structures: replace passive clauses and em-dashes with active verbs.

## Key Patterns
| Slop / AI Tic | Correct Human Alternative |
| --- | --- |
| Let's delve into... | We will look at... |
| In this digital landscape | Now / Today |
| It's not just a tool, it's a partner | [Delete entirely] |
| Furthermore, it is a testament to | This shows that |

## Pitfalls
- **Over-correcting:** Replacing AI clichés with slang or overly casual language can look unprofessional. Strive for clear, direct, and simple text.

## Verification
- Run readability tests to check grade level.
- Read prose aloud to ensure it flows naturally without sounding like an assistant.
