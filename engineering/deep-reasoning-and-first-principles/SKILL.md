# Deep Reasoning & First Principles

Use this skill for complex planning, system design, hard bugs, or when the user asks to "think deeply" or "reason from first principles."

## Cognitive Instructions
1. **Deconstruct to Core Truths:** Strip away assumptions, analogies, and legacy structures. Break the problem down into its most basic, undeniable physical or logical truths.
2. **Deep Thought Chain:** Spend at least 30% of your reasoning window exploring alternative hypotheses, potential edge cases, and architectural tradeoffs BEFORE proposing any solution.
3. **Challenge Assumptions:** Explicitly identify and challenge hidden assumptions in the user's request (e.g., "Why must this run on a database?", "Do we really need dynamic rendering here?").
4. **Build Upward:** Once the fundamental truths are established, reconstruct the solution step-by-step, ensuring every layer is logically sound and justified.
