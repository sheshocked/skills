## When to Use
Create investor pitch decks, sales presentations, partnership proposals, or competition entries. Use when fundraising, pitching to clients, or presenting a business case.

## Core Concepts
- **Deck length**: 10-15 slides max. Investors spend 3-4 minutes on a deck. Every slide must earn its place.
- **Narrative arc**: Problem → Solution → Market → Product → Traction → Team → Ask. This is the universal pitch structure.
- **Visual hierarchy**: One key message per slide. The audience should understand the slide in 3 seconds.
- **Data visualization**: Show trends, not tables. One chart per slide. Label directly on the chart, not in a legend.
- **The ask**: Be specific. "Raising $2M Series A to achieve [specific milestone] in [timeframe]."
- **Design matters**: A poorly designed deck signals a poorly run company. Use consistent fonts, colors, and spacing.

## Workflow
1. **Content first, design second**: Write the content for every slide before touching design tools.
2. **Problem slide**: Articulate the pain point in the customer's language. Use a real quote or statistic.
3. **Solution slide**: Show the product. Screenshots, demos, or mockups. Not bullet points.
4. **Market slide**: TAM/SAM/SOM. Top-down + bottom-up sizing. Investors want to see the market is big enough.
5. **Traction slide**: Revenue, users, growth rate, retention. If pre-revenue: waitlist, pilots, LOIs.
6. **Financials slide**: 3-year projections. Revenue model, key assumptions, path to profitability.
7. **Design and polish**: Consistent branding. Clean layouts. White space is your friend.
8. **Rehearse**: Present to 5 friendly investors before pitching real ones. Get feedback.

## Key Patterns
```markdown
# Pitch Deck Slide-by-Slide

1. TITLE: Company name + one-line description + logo
2. PROBLEM: "X people struggle with Y, costing them Z"
3. SOLUTION: Product screenshot/demo + one-line value prop
4. MARKET OPPORTUNITY: TAM → SAM → SOM with credible sources
5. PRODUCT: Key features, how it works, competitive advantage
6. TRACTION: Key metrics, growth chart, milestones achieved
7. BUSINESS MODEL: How you make money. Pricing, unit economics.
8. COMPETITION: Positioning matrix (you in the top-right quadrant)
9. TEAM: Founders + key hires. Why THIS team wins.
10. FINANCIALS: 3-year projection, key metrics, path to profitability
11. THE ASK: Amount raised, use of funds, milestones this achieves

# Market Sizing Slide
TAM (Total Addressable Market): $X billion
- All potential customers if you had 100% market share
- Source: [Industry report, year]

SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market): $Y million
- The segment you can realistically reach
- Based on: geography, price point, distribution

SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): $Z million
- What you can capture in 3-5 years
- Based on: current traction, growth rate, competitive landscape

# Traction Slide Template
[Large number] [Metric]
↑ [Growth rate] [Timeframe]

Key metrics to show:
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): $XX,XXX
- Month-over-month growth: XX%
- Number of customers: X,XXX
- Retention rate: XX%
- Net Promoter Score: XX
```

## Pitfalls
- **Too many words**: If a slide has more than 30 words, it's a document, not a slide. Condense.
- **No clear ask**: "We're raising" without amount, use of funds, or milestones leaves investors guessing
- **Ignoring competition**: "We have no competition" is a red flag. Show you understand the landscape and how you're different.
- **Vanity metrics**: "1 million page views" means less than "10,000 paying customers." Show metrics that matter.
- **Inconsistent design**: Mixed fonts, different screenshot styles, mismatched colors = unprofessional signal
- **Too many slides**: 20+ slides indicates you can't prioritize. Cut ruthlessly.

## Verification
- 3-second test: Can someone understand each slide in 3 seconds or less?
- Show to 3 people unfamiliar with your business: can they retell your pitch after viewing?
- Every claim has evidence: numbers, charts, testimonials. No unsupported assertions.
- Design consistency: same fonts, colors, spacing, image treatment throughout
- The ask slide is crystal clear: amount, terms, use of funds, expected outcomes
- File size under 5MB (large decks don't get forwarded)
