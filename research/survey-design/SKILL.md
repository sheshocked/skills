## When to Use
Design effective surveys: question wording, scales, sampling, analysis.

## Question Types
1. **Closed-ended**: Yes/No, multiple choice, Likert scale
2. **Open-ended**: Free text for qualitative insights
3. **Ranking**: Order preferences
4. **Matrix**: Rate multiple items on same scale

## Wording Rules
- **Simple**: "How satisfied are you?" not "To what degree..."
- **One topic per question**: Don't combine
- **Neutral**: Avoid leading language
- **Specific**: "In the last 7 days" not "Recently"

## Likert Scale
```markdown
Strongly Disagree (1) - Disagree (2) - Neutral (3) - Agree (4) - Strongly Agree (5)

Example:
"How easy was it to complete checkout?"
1-2: Difficult
3: Neutral
4-5: Easy
```

## Sampling
- **Random**: Every member has equal chance
- **Stratified**: Sample from each subgroup proportionally
- **Convenience**: Easy to reach (biased but fast)

## Pitfalls
- **Survey fatigue**: Keep under 10 minutes
- **Response bias**: Anonymity increases honesty
- **Non-response**: Follow up with non-respondents
- **Leading questions**: Watch for bias in wording

## Verification
- Pilot test with 5-10 people
- Check completion rate
- Verify question clarity
- Analyze response patterns for bias
