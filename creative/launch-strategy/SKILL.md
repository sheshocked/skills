## When to Use
Plan and execute product launches: SaaS releases, app store launches, crowdfunding campaigns, open-source projects, book launches, or any major go-to-market event. Use for pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch phases.

## Core Concepts
- **Launch as a process, not an event**: Pre-launch (4-12 weeks), launch day (peak attention), post-launch (sustain momentum). Most launches fail by focusing only on launch day.
- **Launch assets**: Press kit, product page, demo video, social proof, email sequence, media outreach, community activation.
- **Audience building before launch**: An email list of 1,000+ engaged people is worth more than any ad budget on launch day.
- **Social proof stacking**: Reviews, testimonials, usage stats, media mentions, awards. Stack as many as possible before launch.
- **Scarcity and urgency**: Early-bird pricing, limited beta access, countdown timers. Must be genuine.
- **Launch channels**: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, email list, media outreach, community forums.

## Workflow
1. **Pre-launch (12-8 weeks out)**: Build landing page with email capture. Start building audience. Create teaser content.
2. **Pre-launch (8-4 weeks out)**: Beta program with 50-100 users. Collect testimonials. Build press kit. Create demo video.
3. **Pre-launch (4-2 weeks out)**: Schedule social content. Pitch media. Finalize launch page. Brief partners/influencers.
4. **Pre-launch (1 week out)**: Warm up email list. Final landing page check. Schedule Product Hunt listing. Prepare support.
5. **Launch day**: Ship updates on social every 2-3 hours. Email list blast. Engage with every comment. Monitor metrics.
6. **Post-launch (Week 1)**: Follow up with press. Share results publicly. Address bugs immediately. Thank supporters.
7. **Post-launch (Month 1)**: Convert launch attention into sustained growth. Set up acquisition channels beyond launch buzz.

## Key Patterns
```markdown
# Launch Timeline Template
WEEK -12: Landing page live + email capture
WEEK -10: Teaser content on social (behind-the-scenes)
WEEK -8: Beta program opens (50-100 users)
WEEK -6: Collect beta testimonials + case studies
WEEK -4: Press kit finalized, media outreach begins
WEEK -3: Demo video recorded and polished
WEEK -2: Social content scheduled, partner briefing
WEEK -1: Email list warm-up, final checks
LAUNCH DAY: Coordinated push across all channels
WEEK +1: Follow-up, bug fixes, media follow-up
WEEK +2: Convert attention to retention (onboarding, nurture)
WEEK +4: Launch retrospective, what worked, what didn't

# Product Hunt Launch Checklist
- Maker account set up with complete profile
- Tagline: Under 60 characters, benefit-focused
- Thumbnail: High contrast, readable at small size
- First comment: Your personal story of why you built this
- Gallery images: 5-6 showing key features
- Demo video: 60-90 seconds, no dead time
- First 50 upvotes: Rally your community before posting
- Engage with EVERY comment within 1 hour

# Launch Day Email Sequence
Email 1 (8 AM): "We're live! Here's what's new + special offer"
Email 2 (2 PM): Social proof update — "X people have already signed up"
Email 3 (8 PM): Last chance for launch pricing/discount

# Press Kit Contents
- One-paragraph company description
- One-paragraph product description
- Founder headshots + bios
- Product screenshots (high-res, multiple angles)
- Demo video embed link
- Logo files (SVG, PNG, various sizes)
- Key stats / traction numbers
- Customer quotes (2-3)
- Contact email for press inquiries
```

## Pitfalls
- **No pre-launch audience**: Launching to nobody. Build the email list first. 1,000 subscribers > $10K in ads.
- **One-and-done launch**: A single social post is not a launch strategy. Multi-channel, multi-day push required.
- **Ignoring existing customers**: Launch is for new users AND existing ones. Give current users early access and sharing incentives.
- **Not testing the purchase flow**: Click every button, submit every form, test every payment method on launch day before going live.
- **Post-launch neglect**: The week after launch is when you convert attention into retention. Don't go quiet.
- **Over-promising**: If launch claims don't match the product, reviews will be brutal. Under-promise, over-deliver.

## Verification
- Email list has 1,000+ subscribers before launch day
- All launch assets ready 1 week before launch (not 1 day)
- Beta testimonials collected (minimum 5 with specific quotes)
- Press kit complete with all required assets
- Product Hunt listing optimized with all fields filled
- Launch day social content scheduled (minimum 6 posts)
- Post-launch follow-up sequence ready to send within 24 hours
