## When to Use
Conduct market research: sizing, competitor analysis, customer interviews, trend identification.

## TAM/SAM/SOM Framework
```
TAM (Total Addressable Market) = Everyone who could use this
SAM (Serviceable Available Market) = Everyone you can reach
SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market) = Realistic capture
```

## Competitor Analysis Template
| Factor | Us | Competitor A | Competitor B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $X/mo | $Y/mo | $Z/mo |
| Features | 1,2,3 | 1,2,4 | 2,3,5 |
| Target | SMB | Enterprise | Consumer |
| Weakness | Brand | Price | UX |

## Customer Interview Script
1. Tell me about your current workflow for [X]
2. What tools do you use?
3. What's the biggest frustration?
4. How much time/money do you spend?
5. What would the ideal solution look like?

## Pitfalls
- **Leading questions**: Don't suggest answers
- **Small sample**: Talk to 15-20 people minimum
- **Confirmation bias**: Seek disconfirming evidence
- **Analysis paralysis**: Ship something, iterate

## Verification
- Data sources cited
- Assumptions stated
- Confidence levels assigned
- Counter-arguments addressed
