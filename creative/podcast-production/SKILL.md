## When to Use
Plan, produce, and distribute podcasts: interview shows, solo commentary, narrative storytelling, panel discussions, or branded podcast series. Covers pre-production through post and growth.

## Core Concepts
- **Show format**: Interview (host + guest), solo (monologue), narrative (produced storytelling), panel (multiple voices), or hybrid.
- **Episode structure**: Cold open (hook) → Intro (branding) → Content segments → Outro (CTA). Segments create natural retention checkpoints.
- **Audio quality fundamentals**: Mic technique, room treatment, gain staging. Bad audio = instant skip.
- **Interview prep**: Research guests, prepare 10 questions, but follow curiosity. The best moments are unplanned.
- **Show notes**: SEO-optimized summaries with timestamps, links, and key takeaways. Drives discovery.
- **Cross-promotion**: Guest audiences become your audience. Always have guests share episodes.

## Workflow
1. **Format decision**: Solo = full control, harder to scale. Interview = built-in audience from guests. Narrative = highest production value, most time.
2. **Technical setup**: USB mic (Blue Yeti, Shure MV7) for beginners. XLR (SM7B + interface) for pro. Treat your room with blankets/foam.
3. **Episode planning**: Write a run-of-show doc: segments, timing, talking points, sponsor reads.
4. **Record**: Record locally (each participant records their own audio). Use Riverside.fm or Zencastr for remote.
5. **Edit**: Remove ums, long pauses, tangents. Add intro/outro music, transitions. Use Descript or Hindenburg.
6. **Publish**: Host on Buzzsprout/Transistor/Anchor. Distribute to Apple, Spotify, Google, YouTube (video version).
7. **Promote**: Audiograms (short clips with waveform + subtitles), guest sharing, newsletter, social clips.

## Key Patterns
```markdown
# Run-of-Show Template
Episode #: [number]
Title: [episode title — include keyword for SEO]
Guest: [name, title, company]

[0:00-1:00] COLD OPEN
Pull the most compelling quote from the interview. Place here.

[1:00-2:00] INTRO
Welcome + episode topic + why this guest matters + sponsor read (if applicable)

[2:00-15:00] SEGMENT 1: [Topic]
Key questions:
- [question 1]
- [question 2]
- [follow-up probes]

[15:00-25:00] SEGMENT 2: [Topic]
[Same structure]

[25:00-30:00] RAPID FIRE
Quick questions, fun answers (builds personality)

[30:00-31:00] OUTRO
Key takeaway recap + CTA (subscribe, review, visit guest's site) + next episode teaser

# Podcast Episode Titles (SEO-Optimized)
Template: [Topic]: [Specific Benefit] | [Guest Name] + [Show Name]
Example: "Building Remote Teams: 7 Rules That Actually Work | with Sarah Chen + The Startup Show"

# Audiogram Template (60-sec clip)
- Pull the single best insight from the episode
- Add waveform visualization
- Burn in subtitles (80% of social audio is watched muted)
- End with: "Full episode in bio"
- Post on: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn
```

## Pitfalls
- **Bad audio quality**: No amount of editing fixes a recording made with a laptop mic in an echoey room
- **Episodes too long**: Solo shows: 20-40 min. Interviews: 30-60 min. Over 60 min needs exceptional content
- **Inconsistent publishing**: Weekly is the sweet spot. Biweekly minimum for audience retention
- **Weak guest prep**: Interviewing without research leads to surface-level conversations
- **No show notes**: Wastes SEO opportunity. Every episode should have timestamps, links, and a summary
- **Ignoring YouTube**: Video podcasting is growing. Record video even for audio-only shows

## Verification
- Audio levels: -16 LUFS for stereo, -19 LUFS for mono. No clipping.
- Listen at 1.5x speed: does the content still flow? If not, tighten the edit
- Check analytics: downloads per episode (not just total), completion rate, subscriber growth
- SEO: episode titles contain target keywords? Show notes have 200+ words?
- Feedback: Ask 5 listeners what they'd improve. Watch for patterns
