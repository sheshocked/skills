## When to Use
Plan, produce, and distribute content that drives business goals. Use when building a content calendar, defining content pillars, launching a blog/newsletter, or scaling content operations for a team.

## Core Concepts
- **Content Pillars**: 3-5 core topics your brand owns. Every piece of content maps to one pillar.
- **Content Funnel**: Top (awareness) → Middle (consideration) → Bottom (decision). Different content for each stage.
- **Pillar + Cluster Model**: One comprehensive pillar page + 10-15 supporting cluster posts linking back. Dominates SEO.
- **Content Audit**: Systematic review of existing content to find gaps, winners, and decay.
- **Distribution-First**: Create the distribution plan before writing. Great content with no distribution dies.
- **Content-Market Fit**: When your audience actively seeks out and shares your content. Measured by engagement, not just traffic.

## Workflow
1. **Set business objectives**: Traffic increase? Lead gen? Brand awareness? Each needs different content.
2. **Audience research**: Build 2-3 personas. Map their questions at each funnel stage.
3. **Keyword/topic research**: Use Ahrefs/Semrush for search volume. Use Reddit/Quora for voice-of-customer.
4. **Define pillars**: 3-5 topics that align audience needs with business expertise.
5. **Build content calendar**: 3 months out. Mix: 40% SEO (evergreen), 30% thought leadership, 30% timely/trendy.
6. **Create production workflow**: Brief → Draft → Edit → Design → Publish → Distribute → Update (quarterly).
7. **Measure and iterate**: Monthly review of traffic, engagement, conversions by content type.

```markdown
| W1   | Thought Leadership | Why X Matters in 2026 | Opinion | Middle | Founder | Wed | LinkedIn + Guest post |
| W2   | Conversion | X vs Y Comparison | Blog | Bottom | Writer B | Mon | Retargeting ads + Sales enablement |

# Pillar + Cluster Structure
Pillar: "Complete Guide to Email Marketing" (3000+ words)
Clusters:
- "Email Subject Lines That Get 40%+ Open Rates"
- "Best Time to Send Marketing Emails (Data from 1M Sends)"
- "Email Automation Sequences for E-commerce"
- "How to Clean Your Email List Without Losing Subscribers"
Each cluster links to pillar + to each other.

# Content Brief Template
- Title: [Working title]
- Primary keyword: [keyword + search volume]
- Secondary keywords: [3-5 related terms]
- Target persona: [which persona, what stage]
- Search intent: [informational / transactional / navigational]
- Angle: [what makes this different from existing results]
- Key takeaways: [3 bullet points the reader must remember]
- CTA: [specific action]
- Competitor content to beat: [URLs of top 3 results]
```

## Pitfalls
- **Publishing without distribution**: Plan distribution channels for every piece before writing
- **Ignoring existing content**: Update top-performing posts quarterly. New content ≠ better content
- **Vanity metrics**: Page views don't matter if they don't convert. Track by funnel stage
- **Inconsistency**: Sporadic publishing kills momentum. Consistent quality > sporadic quantity
- **No content briefs**: Writers waste hours guessing what to write. Briefs save 2-3x revision cycles

## Verification
- Run a content audit: categorize all existing content by pillar, funnel stage, and performance
- Check pillar cluster linking: every cluster post links to its pillar and 2+ siblings
- Verify calendar coverage: content across all funnel stages, not just top-of-funnel
- Track content ROI: traffic + leads + conversions attributed to content by source
- Monthly content review: what worked, what didn't, what to double down on
