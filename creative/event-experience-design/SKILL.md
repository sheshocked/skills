## When to Use
Design and execute events: conferences, workshops, meetups, product launches, brand activations, virtual events, or hybrid experiences. Use for planning, engagement design, logistics, and post-event optimization.

## Core Concepts
- **Event journey**: Pre-event (anticipation) → During (engagement) → Post-event (reflection + action). Design all three phases.
- **Engagement design**: Passive audiences disengage. Build interaction into every segment: polls, Q&A, breakout groups, activities.
- **Session formats**: Keynote (inspiration), workshop (skill-building), panel (diverse perspectives), networking (connection), demo (proof).
- **The "sticky" moment**: Every event needs one unforgettable moment — a surprise, performance, announcement, or emotional peak.
- **Hybrid considerations**: Remote attendees need their own experience, not a camera pointed at a stage.
- **Event metrics**: Attendance rate, engagement rate (interactions per attendee), satisfaction (NPS), post-event conversion.

## Workflow
1. **Define objectives**: Why are we hosting this? Lead gen? Community building? Product launch? Education? Define success metrics.
2. **Budget allocation**: Venue (30-40%), production (20-25%), content/speakers (10-15%), marketing (10-15%), contingency (10%).
3. **Program design**: Block out the agenda. Mix session types. Build in breaks (10 min minimum between sessions).
4. **Speaker/content curation**: Book speakers 3-6 months out. Provide briefs, not scripts. Rehearsals are non-negotiable.
5. **Venue/logistics**: Capacity, AV requirements, accessibility, catering, signage, Wi-Fi, parking.
6. **Marketing/registration**: Event page, social promotion, email campaigns, partner co-marketing, early-bird pricing.
7. **Execution day**: Run-of-show doc, team roles, tech checks, audience flow management.
8. **Post-event**: Survey (within 24 hours), follow-up email (within 48 hours), content distribution, lead routing.

## Key Patterns
```markdown
# Event Agenda Template (Full-Day Conference)
08:00-09:00  Registration + Coffee (networking time)
09:00-09:15  Welcome + Housekeeping
09:15-10:00  Keynote: [Topic]
10:00-10:15  Break
10:15-11:00  Panel: [Topic] (3-4 speakers + moderator)
11:00-11:15  Break
11:15-12:00  Workshop A / Workshop B (parallel tracks)
12:00-13:00  Lunch + Networking Activity
13:00-13:45  fireside Chat: [Topic]
13:45-14:00  Break
14:00-14:45  Demo / Product Showcase
14:45-15:00  Break
15:00-15:45  Workshop C / Workshop D (parallel tracks)
15:45-16:00  Closing Keynote + Announcements
16:00-17:00  Happy Hour / Networking

# Engagement Touchpoints
- Live polls during keynotes (Slido, Mentimeter)
- Q&A boards for every session (not just "any questions?" from the stage)
- Photo opportunities with branded backdrops
- Networking matching (Bumble Bizz style for B2B events)
- Gamification: points for attending sessions, visiting sponsors, posting on social
- Surprise and delight: random prize drops, celebrity guest, unexpected performance

# Post-Event Follow-Up Sequence
Email 1 (24 hrs): Thank you + survey link + session recordings
Email 2 (48 hrs): Key takeaways blog post + speaker slides
Email 3 (1 week): Exclusive content/offers for attendees
Email 4 (2 weeks): Save the date for next event
```

## Pitfalls
- **Overloading the agenda**: Back-to-back sessions with no breaks leads to burnout and disengagement
- **Ignoring remote attendees**: Hybrid events that treat remote as second-class lose that audience
- **No networking design**: "Networking lunch" is not a strategy. Create structured opportunities for connection
- **Tech failures**: Test every AV element, Wi-Fi capacity, and streaming setup 24 hours before
- **Skipping the survey**: Without feedback, you can't improve. Send within 24 hours while memory is fresh
- **No post-event follow-up**: Leads go cold in 48 hours. Have your follow-up sequence ready before the event.

## Verification
- Agenda has breaks every 60-90 minutes and variety in session formats
- Every session has a clear learning outcome or takeaway
- Hybrid setup tested: remote attendees can see, hear, and participate equally
- Run-of-show document: minute-by-minute timeline with assigned team responsibilities
- Post-event survey sent within 24 hours with response rate target > 30%
- Follow-up sequence converts attendees to next action (demo, signup, purchase)
