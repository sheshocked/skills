## When to Use
Build, grow, and monetize an email newsletter. Use when launching a new newsletter, optimizing an existing one, increasing open rates, reducing churn, or building a media business around email.

## Core Concepts
- **Newsletter = product**: Treat it like a SaaS. The "product" is the email. The "customers" are subscribers.
- **Open rate benchmarks**: Good: 30-40%. Great: 40-50%. Industry average varies (media: 20-25%, B2B: 22-28%).
- **Subject line psychology**: Curiosity gaps, numbers, specificity, personalization, urgency. Test relentlessly.
- **Welcome sequence**: 3-5 emails over 7-14 days. Set expectations, deliver value, build habit.
- **List quality > list size**: 1,000 engaged subscribers > 100,000 inactive ones. Prune regularly.
- **Monetization paths**: Sponsorships (CPM-based), premium tier (Substack/Memberful), products/courses, affiliate.

## Workflow
1. **Niche down**: The tighter the niche, the higher the engagement. "Marketing tips" = crowded. "B2B SaaS pricing strategies" = opportunity.
2. **Choose platform**: Substack (simplicity + built-in network), Beehiiv (growth tools + monetization), ConvertKit (creator-focused), Mailchimp (beginner-friendly).
3. **Set cadence**: Weekly is standard. Daily works for news-heavy niches. Don't commit to more than you can sustain.
4. **Write consistently**: Same day, same time. Readers build habits around your schedule.
5. **Grow subscribers**: Lead magnets, cross-promotions (Swapstack), social media, referral programs.
6. **Engage**: Reply to every email. Ask questions. Make readers feel seen.
7. **Optimize**: A/B test subject lines. Segment by engagement. Prune cold subscribers quarterly.

## Key Patterns
```markdown
# Subject Line Templates
- [Number] [Specific Outcome]: "7 landing page changes that doubled my conversion rate"
- Question: "Are you making this pricing mistake?"
- Curiosity gap: "The one thing I wish I knew before launching"
- Personal: "[First name], this changed how I think about [topic]"
- Urgency: "Last chance: [specific deadline]"

# Welcome Sequence (5 emails)
Email 1 (Immediate): Welcome + lead magnet delivery + what to expect
Email 2 (Day 2): Your story/why you write this newsletter
Email 3 (Day 4): Your best piece of content (proof of value)
Email 4 (Day 7): Ask what they're struggling with (engagement signal)
Email 5 (Day 10): Premium offer or referral program invitation

# Newsletter Template Structure
Subject: [Curiosity-driven title]
Preview text: [Second hook, not a repeat of subject]
---
[Opening hook — 1-2 sentences, personal or surprising]
---
[Main content — 3-5 sections, each with a clear takeaway]
---
[CTA — one clear action: reply, click, share, upgrade]
---
[PS line — often the most-read part. Add a bonus link or personal note]

# Referral Program Structure
Tier 1 (3 referrals): Exclusive template or resource
Tier 2 (10 referrals): Access to private community
Tier 3 (25 referrals): Free product or consulting call
Tool: SparkLoop or Beehiiv's built-in referral system
```

## Pitfalls
- **Writing for everyone**: The broader the audience, the less anyone cares. Niche is not limiting, it's focusing.
- **Inconsistent sending**: Skip a week and you lose 10-20% of your open rate. Consistency > quality.
- **Ignoring the subject line**: It's the most important line you write. 80% of people decide based on subject alone.
- **No segmentation**: Treating every subscriber the same wastes relevance. Segment by interest and engagement.
- **Monetizing too early**: Build to 1,000+ engaged subscribers before introducing sponsors.
- **Not asking for replies**: Reply rate signals to email providers (Gmail, Outlook) that your emails are wanted.

## Verification
- Open rate above 30% for 4 consecutive issues
- Click-through rate above 3%
- Reply rate above 1% (strong signal for deliverability)
- Unsubscribe rate below 0.5% per issue
- Welcome sequence completion rate above 60%
- At least one growth lever active (referral program, cross-promo, lead magnet)
